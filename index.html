About

I am pursuing an Integrated Master's in Aerospace Engineering and a micro-specialization in Artificial Intelligence and Applications at IIT Kharagpur. My work so far includes spacecraft trajectory optimization, attitude control using sliding mode control, and rendezvous and docking. I have a deep interest in dynamical systems theory, since the time I encountered invariant manifolds and upcoming missions for Earth-Moon transport. At IIT Kharagpur, I have worked with the Space Flight Mechanics and Controls Group and the Student Satellite Program.

Safety-critical control for a UAV-payload system

Indian Institute of Science, 2024

During my internship at the Formal Control and Autonomous Systems Lab, I worked with collision-cone control barrier functions for collision avoidance during trajectory tracking.

Spacecraft attitude control using DGVSCMGs

IIT Kharagpur, 2025–present

I am building spacecraft attitude-control simulations that include double-gimbal variable-speed control moment gyroscope dynamics. I have implemented sliding-mode and adaptive sliding-mode controllers for stabilization, speed regulation and momentum management.

Single-motor-failure quadcopter control

ideaForge and IIT Kharagpur, 2024

Our team developed a fault-tolerant control strategy to stabilize a quadcopter after the loss of one motor. The project won Gold Medal at the 13th Inter IIT Tech Meet, held at IIT Bombay.

Unmanned rover for sample pick-and-place

ISRO Robotics Challenge, 2023–2024

Our team designed the a rocker-bogie type rover for sample pick-and-place activity in an arena with craters and obstacles of different sizes. The aim of this competition was to invite ideas ISRO's Chandrayaan-4 mission to the Moon. We used static and dynamic simulations to study load capacity, gradeability, and terrain handling. We reached the first stage of the semi-final round (Quals-1).
